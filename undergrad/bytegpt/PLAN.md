I want some controls at the top of the page for temperature and top_k (setting to 0 disables it), and the left side should be a custom text input area (capped at 4096 chars). Make the up/down arrows change layer number, and left/right change head # for easy scrolling with keyboard. The attention scores should be displayed in the form of writing out the prompt before this point, and highlighting with color scaling putting the areas of high attention in green and low attention in red. As you scroll through layers/attn heads you will be able to see the attention scores change.

Structure the code nicely, you may create custom logic and components, but make sure to organize them such that they dont clutter the directories (this is my blog containing a ton of other stuff!).
